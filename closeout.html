Questions

What teams usually ask.

Is this a separate custom app just for us?

You receive a private workspace configured for your company on one maintained platform. This gives you your branding and workflow without the cost and risk of maintaining a separate software codebase.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

No. We start alongside your current process and use a real project to decide what should move into the workspace.

Who owns our information?

Your company owns the information it submits. We operate and maintain the platform, and we will document export and deletion terms in the service agreement.

Can we store medical information?

No. The current service is for operational closeout details and is not designed for protected health information or medical records.

What does resolved mean?

An item is resolved when its final destination, responsible party, completion date, and any available supporting note or proof are recorded.

Can we start with a current project?

Yes. A live project is the best way to configure and validate your workflow.
