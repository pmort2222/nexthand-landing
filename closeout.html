Questions

What teams usually ask.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

No. The pilot works alongside your current process. You can share a spreadsheet, notes, or an export from the system you already use.

Who communicates with clients and vendors?

Your team remains in control of every relationship. We maintain the closeout record and flag the follow up your team needs to complete.

What does resolved mean?

An item is resolved when its final destination, responsible party, completion date, and any available supporting note or proof are recorded.

Can we start with a current project?

Yes. A live project is the best way to test the service. We begin with a short consultation to confirm scope and fit.
