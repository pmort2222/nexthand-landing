Founding pilot terms

Effective September 8, 2026. These terms describe the current Next Hand Closeout founding partner service. A signed pilot agreement may add or replace terms for a specific customer.

Current founding offer

$750 one-time implementation plus $149 per month, with a three-month initial subscription. The implementation includes workflow discovery, company branding, workspace configuration, first-project setup, and a team launch session for up to five users.

The service

Next Hand Closeout provides a hosted operational workspace for tracking projects, belongings, destinations, responsible people, deadlines, exceptions, completion records, and related non-sensitive attachments. Features may evolve during the founding pilot.

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide accurate account and workspace information.
  • Use the service only for lawful business purposes.
  • Control team access and promptly remove former workers or unauthorized users.
  • Maintain appropriate independent records for legal, tax, appraisal, insurance, accounting, or payment purposes.
  • Obtain any permissions needed to upload client or third-party information.
  • Avoid medical, financial, regulated, or highly sensitive records.

What is not included

The service does not provide physical moving, selling, hauling, appraisal, valuation, legal advice, tax advice, insurance services, property custody, escrow, or payment handling. Complex data migration, integrations, custom automation, additional users, and separate branded app-store releases require a separate written scope.

The implementation is accepted when the configured workspace, first project, agreed branding, and team launch session are delivered. Any different acceptance criteria should be written into the customer pilot agreement before work begins.
