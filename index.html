Your first 5 minutes

Here's exactly what to do after you download the app.

0:00

Open the app and tap "Add"

Hit the + button at the bottom. Type in the name of something you're selling, set a price, and save. Don't overthink it — you can always edit later.

Start with your 3 most important items. You can add the rest later.
1:00

Add your first buyer

Tap into any item, scroll to the Buyers section, and tap "+ Add Buyer." Enter their name, where they messaged you (Facebook, OfferUp, etc.), and how serious they seem.

Already have people messaging you? Add them now. Even just names and "Maybe real" is enough to start.
2:30

Check your Buyer Queue

Tap the Queue tab at the bottom. This shows every buyer across all your items, sorted by who needs your attention first. Overdue meetups at the top, new messages below.

3:30

Take action from the Home screen

Go back to Home. The app now tells you exactly what to do — follow up with a buyer, confirm a meetup, or lock in a serious buyer. One tap to act on each one.

Use the "Message" button to copy pre-written texts like "Are you still interested?" — then paste directly into Marketplace or text.
5:00

You're set up. Now just follow the app.

Every time someone messages you about an item, add them as a buyer. Every time you open the app, do what it tells you. That's the whole system.

The result?

You stop guessing who's serious. You stop forgetting to follow up. You sell your stuff faster with less stress.

How to install

It's an Android app — not on the Play Store yet, so here's how to get it.

1

Tap "Download for Android" on your phone

Make sure you open this page on your Android phone, not a computer.

2

Wait for the download to finish

The app file is about 81 MB, so give it a moment. Then open it from your notifications or your Downloads folder.

3

Allow "Install from unknown sources"

Android will ask for permission since it's not from the Play Store. Tap "Settings" then toggle on "Allow from this source." This is normal and safe.

4

Open the app and start adding items

That's it. No account needed, no sign-up. The app works entirely on your phone.
